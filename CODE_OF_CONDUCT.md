# Code of conduct

Swiss Scooters aims to be a welcoming, practical, and respectful project.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate constructively,
respect different backgrounds and levels of experience, accept good-faith
feedback, and keep disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are
not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `swiss-scooters@plhery.com`. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that violate these
expectations. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
